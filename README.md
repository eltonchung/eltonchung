### [OpenPlanTeam｜專案規劃工作台 · Project Planning Workspace](https://github.com/eltonchung/OpenPlanTeam-Showcase)

`React` `TypeScript` `Zustand` `Vite` · **本機試行版展示 / Local-trial showcase**

以 OpenPlan 為基礎延伸開發的瀏覽器專案規劃工具，整合甘特圖、任務看板、資源負載、基準比較與交接報告。加入繁體中文工作流程、匯入驗證與報表功能，讓時程、資源衝突及交接資訊更容易掌握；並非即時多人協作服務。

### [Redmine Hours Helper｜工時補填助手](https://github.com/eltonchung/redmine-hours-helper)

`Python` `Tkinter` `REST API` `GUI + CLI` · **MIT 授權 / MIT licensed**

先預覽每日已填工時與目標差額，確認後再補登到指定議題。桌面與命令列共用邏輯，包含寫入前重新查詢、日期規則及進度回饋，讓重複行政操作有明確的檢查步驟。

A desktop and command-line helper that previews daily Redmine time-entry gaps before submitting confirmed entries to a selected issue. Shared logic, pre-write rechecks, calendar rules, and progress feedback make repetitive administration easier to review.

### [iBeaconLog｜iBeacon 進出紀錄工具 · Entry & Exit Logger](https://github.com/eltonchung/BeaconLogger-Showcase)

`iOS` `SwiftUI` `SwiftData` `Core Location` · **產品與技術展示 / Product & engineering showcase**

為指定 iBeacon 範圍建立進入與離開紀錄，整合具名 Beacon、通知、冷卻與防重複機制。資料留在 iPhone 本機，不需要帳號或後端，也不建立 GPS 移動路線。

An iOS app that logs entry into and exit from specified iBeacon regions, with named beacons, notifications, cooldowns, and duplicate prevention. Records stay on the iPhone, without an account, backend, or GPS route history.
